The idea

Technology for emergency healthcare in West Bengal

Emergency Bed Tracker – West Bengal is a concept platform designed to make hospital bed discovery faster and easier across the state's 23 districts.

A future version could connect hospitals directly, letting staff update availability in real time — so patients, families and ambulance crews always see the latest picture. For now, every number on this site is demo data for illustration only.
